# A student's Enrollment is not tied to the Purchase that granted it

A student has one Enrollment per course, whether it came from an Individual Purchase or a Claimed Seat, and the Enrollment doesn't record which. A Refund Request, by contrast, belongs to one specific Purchase, which the buyer chooses: Individual Purchases from the course page, Team Licences from the team page. So approving a refund on an Individual Purchase must check whether the student also holds a Claimed Seat for that course, and leave their Enrollment alone if they do.
