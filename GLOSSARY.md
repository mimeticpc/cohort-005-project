# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to Students. This glossary covers the people involved, buying courses and getting money back after a Purchase.

## Language

### People

**Platform Admin**:
A member of Cadence staff who runs the platform. Only a Platform Admin decides Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), the team, Staff

**Student**:
Someone with an Enrollment on a course, including one whose Enrollment has ended. A person is a Student of a particular course, not a Student in general.
_Avoid_: Learner, User, Customer

**Instructor**:
Someone who builds and edits the courses they own and sees who is Enrolled on them. An Instructor has no say over Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Teacher, Author, Creator

**Team Admin**:
Someone who buys and manages a Team Licence. Buying one doesn't make them a Student of that course, and they have no say over Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own)

### Purchases

**Purchase**:
A single payment for a course. It is either an Individual Purchase or a Team Licence.
_Avoid_: Order, Transaction

**Individual Purchase**:
A Purchase someone makes for themselves. It gives the buyer an Enrollment.

**Team Licence**:
A Purchase a Team Admin makes for others. It grants Seats, not the course itself; the buyer can't watch the course through it.
_Avoid_: Team purchase, Bulk purchase

**Seat**:
One place on a Team Licence that someone can claim to get an Enrollment.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Code, Voucher

**Enrollment**:
Someone's place on a course, which lets them watch it and makes them a Student of that course. A person has at most one Enrollment per course, whether it came from an Individual Purchase or a Claimed Seat.
_Avoid_: Place on a course, Access

**Ended Enrollment**:
An Enrollment that no longer lets its holder watch the course, because a Refund Request ended it. Their progress on the course is kept, and buying the course again reactivates it.
_Avoid_: Lapsed, Inactive, Revoked

**Unclaimed Seat**:
A Seat nobody has claimed yet.

**Claimed Seat**:
A Seat someone has claimed. It belongs to them, so a Team Licence with any Claimed Seat can't be refunded.

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
The buyer's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for one specific Purchase. A Platform Admin approves or declines it. One Purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Platform Admin has decided yet. The buyer keeps their Enrollment while it is open, and a Team Licence's Unclaimed Seats can't be claimed.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has accepted. Approving it ends what the Purchase granted (the buyer's Enrollment for an Individual Purchase; its Unclaimed Seats, which can then never be claimed, for a Team Licence) and leads to a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has turned down. Nothing the Purchase granted is lost, it has no Refund, and the buyer may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to the buyer after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund whose Refund Request has been approved, where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the buyer.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.

**Refund Window**:
How long after a Purchase the buyer can raise a Refund Request. Once it closes, they can't raise one.

**Completion Limit**:
The share of a course's lessons the buyer can have as Completed Lessons and still raise a Refund Request. Past it, they can't raise one.

**Completed Lesson**:
A lesson a Student has marked as done, whether or not they Watched it.
_Avoid_: Finished, Done

**Watched**:
How much of a course a Student has seen: the furthest point reached in each lesson, added up. Skipping ahead counts. Opening a lesson without playing it doesn't.
_Avoid_: Viewed, Progress

**Watch Limit**:
The most of a course a buyer can have Watched and still raise a Refund Request. Past it, the buyer can't raise one.
_Avoid_: Watch cap, Viewing threshold
